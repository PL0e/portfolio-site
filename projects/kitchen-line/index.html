FAQ

Pickleball paddles & balls FAQ

What paddle should a beginner buy?

Start with a mid-weight composite or fiberglass paddle. It is forgiving on off-center hits and easy on the arm while you learn.

What is the difference between indoor and outdoor balls?

Outdoor balls have about 40 smaller holes and harder plastic to handle wind and rough courts. Indoor balls have about 26 larger holes and a softer feel.

Does core thickness matter?

Thicker cores (around 16 mm) feel softer and give more control. Thinner cores (around 13 mm) feel livelier and add power.
