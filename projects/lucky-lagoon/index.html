Responsible gaming, built in

Games should stay entertainment. These responsible gaming tools are on by default and easy to find.

  • Deposit limits

    Daily, weekly, and monthly caps that take effect immediately and only loosen after 24 hours.

  • Reality checks

    A reminder every 30 minutes showing time played and net result for the session.

  • Cooling-off

    Pause your account for a day to six weeks with one tap, no questions asked.

  • Self-exclusion

    Close your account for six months or longer and block all promotional messages.

If gambling stops being fun, talk to someone you trust and reach out to a local support service. This concept takes no bets and holds no money.
