03 Lighting guide

The right warm light for every room

  • 2700K

    Living room

    Layer a floor lamp with a table lamp at eye level for a warm, even glow.

  • 2200–2700K

    Bedroom

    Go warmer and dimmer at night. Amber light is easier on your eyes before sleep.

  • 3000–4000K

    Workspace

    Neutral, focused task light reduces eye strain while you read or work.
